# Example: scripted documentary

Replace bracketed paths. Jane Doe and John Doe are fictional examples. Settings describe this example, not universal project defaults.

> Help me create a warm, story-driven documentary in DaVinci Resolve using the workflow skills. Keep a trackable plan. Ask for missing guidance affecting the edit and continue independent preparation while waiting.
>
> **Script:** Read [script CSV or Sheets link], separating dialogue, B-roll suggestions and production instructions. Follow the approved story unless I give a runtime. Jane Doe's interview belongs after “One such story is Jane.” John Doe follows the script. Confirm source files/takes and identities from my media map; do not guess surnames or student year. Trim mistakes, long pauses and restarts without changing meaning.
>
> **Media and storage:** Root is [project root]; footage in assets/ROLL01 onward; recordings in assets/WAV; approved music in [music folder]. Some media is Dropbox online-only. Free space is [available GB]. Budget bounded downloads, proxies, cache, intermediates and renders. Verify ungraded proxies and link natively, then Make online-only only verified cloud-backed originals with usable local proxies. Never delete originals for space. Put new intake in a new ROLL folder.
>
> **Edit and B-roll:** Use a new versioned timeline at exact 24 fps; name drafts YYYY.MM.DD Community Story V1. Preserve versions and save .drp/.drt milestones in projects. Match cutaways to the spoken person/event/place/action. Jane's campus material starts after her introduction. Use coherent action sequences, varied landscape footage and no repeated setups. Slow B-roll when source motion/rate supports it; stabilize where useful. Hide interview edits with relevant B-roll or modest original-resolution punch-ins. Keep the face if coverage is irrelevant.
>
> **Gaps:** Sharing and ending montage recordings are missing. Preserve their slots. Add draft text on black at 50% opacity identifying the missing asset; maintain a version-relative footage-request CSV with priority and context. Do not present a later conversation or different event as the described scene.
>
> **Text:** Use restrained fades, large readable intro/outro cards and editable lower thirds. Use confirmed names and placeholders for unknown student years. Ask for approved ESV wording/reference for the harvest verse. Avoid repeated film-name labels. Keep lower thirds clear of phrase-based, usually single-line editable captions. Suppress duplicate cues only where the full text is conveyed by a card and I approve that treatment. Add a subtle native Color FX vignette, not a PNG.
>
> **Color:** Verify profiles and scopes. For verified Sony S-Log3, use the licensed Leeming Athena III transform and its guide once. Do not reapply it to baked grades. Match other cameras through their actual pipeline, including HDR. Verify original/proxy range consistency, balance shots and save reference frames. Request originals for advanced work; report unrecoverable clipping/noise.
>
> **Audio:** Sync interviews/narration to correct external WAVs with waveforms and beginning/middle/end lip-sync checks. Recordings may be outdoor 32-bit float WAVs. Keep speakers, ambience and music on separate organized tracks. Use appropriate low-cut, restrained isolation and cleanup. Fit hopeful music to the story and keep quiet speech intelligible. Final encoded true peak must be at or below −2 dBTP; report integrated loudness separately.
>
> **Delivery:** Make a 1080p24 sharing MP4 with visibly burned captions and retain editable cues. Preserve earlier exports; read back settings and render only intended jobs. Check metadata, full decode, transitions/titles/captions/color, encoded peaks and sync. Use native UI for unsupported controls and screenshots for visual checks. If playback stalls, use rendered review samples and disclose limits. Verify Dropbox upload before claiming cloud availability. Report missing assets and outstanding full listening/calibrated review.
