# Event recap example

Copy and adapt; the order and ending below are choices for this example.

~~~text
Use the DaVinci workflow skills to make an upbeat event recap in my open
project from the footage, supplied music and animated title in this folder.
Keep my current timeline tweaks and make a recoverable revision.

Group games and crafts, then prizes, announcements and food. Choose active
moments and reactions; keep the landing of throws and the payoff of games.
Vary the shot lengths around musical beats and phrases instead of cutting
every clip to one duration. Review the action in motion before trimming.

Include the group saying thank you near the end, with clear camera audio,
then the supplied animated end card. Fit the music to this ending and keep
the words intact. Preserve my later source slips, crops, leveling and fades.

Check phone HDR interpretation and output conversion before grading; match
shots without a universal phone preset. Review channel mapping and the
encoded sign-off. Use shot/action notes for my review and identify anything
you could not verify by playback or listening.
~~~
